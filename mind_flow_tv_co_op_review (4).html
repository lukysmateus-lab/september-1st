Verb Patterns & Rules PROVA PERIÓDICA TOPIC
1. GERUND (-ing)

After verbs: enjoy, avoid, finish, practise, dislike, mind, keep, suggest, consider.

Ex: I enjoy playing games.
2. INFINITIVE (to + verb)

After verbs: want, decide, hope, promise, agree, refuse, plan, learn, offer.

Ex: She decided to study abroad.
3. AFTER PREPOSITIONS

After prepositions (about, at, in, of, for) always use GERUND (-ing)!

Ex: Good at speaking, interested in buying.
